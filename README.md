## ✨ Key Features

### 🛡️ 1. Zero Ads & Automatic SponsorBlock
- **100% Ad-Free:** Connects directly to high-speed media distribution streams. No pre-roll, mid-roll, post-roll, or banner ads ever load.
- **SponsorBlock Integration:** Automatically skips embedded sponsor segments (*"A quick word from our sponsor..."*), intros, and outro filler in real time.

### ⚡ 2. On-Screen Speed Pill Controls
- Dedicated **`[ – ]` `1.0x` `[ + ]`** speed widget directly in the player utility bar.
- Step through speeds from **`0.25x` to `3.0x`** with single taps.
- **1-Tap Reset:** Tap the center speed text to instantly snap back to **`1.0x` Normal** speed.

### ⏸️ 3. Persistent Pause Screen & Central Action Card
- Screen controls remain visible **permanently when paused** — no rushed auto-hiding.
- Centered high-contrast action card for:
  - **Thumbs Up 👍 (Like / Liked)**
  - **Thumbs Down 👎 (Dislike)**
  - **Share ↗️ (Native Android share)**
  - **AI Summary ✨ (Gemini video breakdowns)**
  - **Offline Download ⬇️**

### 🤖 4. Gemini AI Video Summaries
- Get structured, instant summaries, key points, and final takeaways from any video with one tap.
- Timestamped note-taking linked directly to video playback.

### 🚀 5. Apple HLS Master Stream Engine
- Zero buffering delay: Adaptive Apple HLS streaming ensures immediate playback without audio/video drift or HTTP connection starvation.

### 💾 6. High-Quality Offline Downloads
- Download full HD videos with hardware-muxed audio for offline listening and flight mode.

### 🖼️ 7. Picture-in-Picture & Background Playback
- Seamless multitasking with system Picture-in-Picture (PiP) and notification media controls.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **UI Framework:** [Jetpack Compose](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/compose) with Material You dynamic design
- **Media Engine:** [AndroidX Media3 (ExoPlayer)](https://developer.android.com/media/media3) with Apple HLS & DASH pipeline
- **Networking:** [OkHttp 4](https://square.github.io/okhttp/) with high-performance connection pooling
- **Local Persistence:** [Room SQLite](https://developer.android.com/training/data-storage/room) with Stale-While-Revalidate (SWR) cache
- **Image Loading:** [Coil Compose](https://coil-kt.github.io/coil/compose/)
- **AI Intelligence:** Google Gemini API for instant video extraction and structuring

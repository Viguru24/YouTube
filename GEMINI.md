### Workspace Rules & Preferences

- **Environment:** Windows OS. Use PowerShell for all scripts.
- **Workflow:**
  - **Local-First:** ALWAYS work locally (`localhost`) First by default.
  - **Deployment:** NEVER deploy to production (Netlify/Vercel) or push to GitHub unless explicitly instructed.
  - **Cloud Storage & Sync:** NEVER copy, upload, reference, or update Google Drive or OneDrive. All Google Drive and OneDrive sync is permanently discontinued.
  - **Device Updates:** ONLY update or install to the phone or connected physical device when explicitly told to do so by the user.
  - **Safety:** Always backup databases before risky migrations.
- **Aesthetics:** Prioritize "rich," "premium," and "dynamic" design (glassmorphism, animations, high-quality UI). Avoid generic/basic looks.
- **Communication:** Be concise, helpful, and proactive. Always make links clickable.
